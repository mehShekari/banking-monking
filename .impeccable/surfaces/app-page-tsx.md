## Direction contract

THESIS: One continuous product film of one card, with scroll as the playhead. The card's engraved circuit is the map of the holder's path from knowledge to impact. The film refuses the fintech stack (hero, feature grid, pricing, FAQ), any navbar, and any "best bank card" framing.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Ground:** a deep navy-black void (#04060d to #111c3d), the card's own brushed navy metal, and light as the only accent: cold white (#e8f1ff) for rims and sweeps, electric blue (#7cc4ff) for living circuits and the path.
- **Type:** Reem Kufi for display, a square Kufic whose straight strokes match the traces; Vazirmatn for small text.
- **Layout:** text sits in the void like film titles, with no boxes and no cards; labels are pinned to 3D anchors. One hairline progress rail.

STORY:
1. **Chosen:** a field of faint talent; one point is selected while three braided strands of light (Daneshmand, Bank Sina, Green Bank) descend onto it and cinch into it; its light becomes the card's edge. The hook line is «انتخاب شده‌اید، چون متفاوتید».
2. The card turns out of the blade while its face is assembled from light (the circuit first), the calligraphy is written by light, then a light sweep: the card is for Daneshmand's selected community and pays prizes, stipends and grants.
3. **The Path:** a pulse born from the name climbs a lit trace across the real circuit through seven stages, from «راهی‌شو» to اثر, with the camera tracking it like a macro shot; at each stage the road to the next is pre-lit (staged funding). Then the achievement beat: a wave re-runs the whole path and its nodes lift into a constellation — «دستاورد بیشتر می‌تواند جایگاه بالاتر بسازد».
4. **The Doors:** the card becomes a blade of light again and flies through doors made of its own circuit, which open as it passes; the camera follows.
5. **Finale:** «از دانش، تا اثر.» The talent field returns and the visitor's pointer is the chosen point of light. The card is hands-on: drag to turn it, the light follows the cursor, and press-and-hold charges the circuit until it bursts. One CTA.
6. **End credits:** the brochure's privileges roll up like film credits over the dimmed scene, worded as the brochure words them (gradual, never promised), ending on «پژوهش‌یار هستید. انتخاب شده‌اید، چون متفاوتید.» and the CTA.
Act changes fracture briefly along the card's circuit; fast scrolling streaks the light. Opt-in synthesized sound (off by default).

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- A full-bleed void with letterbox bars and thousands of faint drifting points.
- Centered credits: the three issuers appear one by one, each with its strand of light, then «تقدیم می‌کنند».
- One point brightens, travels to the centre and stretches into a vertical blade of light (the card edge-on).
- The hook line rises at the inline-start lower third, and the scroll cue sits inside the bottom bar.
- The rail sits at the inline-end edge, and a small persistent CTA link at the inline-start top.

FORM: The user's storyboard (pinned), rebuilt as five acts plus credits on one Theatre sequence (42.2 s, including a 2.2 s achievement hold). The signature interaction is the path: a pulse drawn along the circuit with stage titles revealed by its light, then doors made of the circuit opening for the card. Seed key: none (pinned brief).
